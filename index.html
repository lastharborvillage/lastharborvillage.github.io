                    How to move in

                    1. 1
                      Follow and you move in

                      Every follower lives in Last Harbor: you get a tent on the beach the next day.

                    2. 2
                      Comment for your own house

                      A name, a job and one thing about your villager. "Greta, a baker who is scared of bread" works.

                    3. 3
                      Have your say

                      Every day ends with one choice. Comment the word, and the next day shows what the village picked. Pause and find yourself, then find your house here.
