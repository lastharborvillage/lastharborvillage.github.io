Privacy Policy

Last Harbor Publisher · last updated 10 November 2026

Who we are

Last Harbor Publisher is a private tool used only by the creator of the Last Harbor series to publish their own animated videos to their own accounts (YouTube, Instagram and TikTok). It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in with it. Questions: cctv.shorts64@gmail.com.

TikTok

The tool uses TikTok's Login Kit, Content Posting API and Display API, only for the creator's own Last Harbor TikTok account. TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply.

What data the tool accesses

  • Basic profile of the creator's own TikTok account (account ID, display name, username, avatar): to check it publishes to the right account.
  • Account statistics (follower, like and video counts) and the list of the account's own videos with their view, like, comment and share counts: shown on the creator's private statistics page.
  • Video upload: to send the creator's own finished episodes to their TikTok account, as drafts or posts.

The tool does not read comments, messages, contacts or anything about other TikTok users.

How the data is used and stored

  • Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the creator's own private server, never in public files.
  • Data is used only for the purposes above. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

YouTube and Instagram

The same tool publishes the series on YouTube (it uses YouTube API Services; the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy apply) and on Instagram (Meta's terms apply). There it reads public comments on the series' own videos, so a comment such as "Nick, fisherman" can become a villager in a later episode; the commenter's ID is only used to give each person one villager and is never shown.

Deleting your data

The creator can remove the tool's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services; the stored tokens are then useless and are deleted on request. Anyone who wants data about them removed can email the address above; it is deleted within 30 days.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
